### Open Source

I used to do lots of Open Source stuff in the late 1990s/early 2000s.
You can find my code in the released versions
of [Pure-FTPd](http://www.pureftpd.org/project/pure-ftpd), [Gnu Privacy Guard](http://www.gnupg.org/),
official [Exim](https://exim.org/) RPM spec, and more.
I also used to have my own public patches
for [Cyrus-SASL](http://cyrusimap.web.cmu.edu/mediawiki/index.php/Cyrus_SASL), [mod_auth_mysql](http://modauthmysql.sourceforge.net/) amongst others.
I also published Perl, JavaScript and other custom RPMs.

In 2016, I went back to my roots to work for an open source company. Check out
my [GitHub](https://github.com/nicdoye) and
my [gists](https://gist.github.com/nicdoye).

I've often thought of writing a DNS Server, but it's never happened.
A sketch of the design [is available](https://nicdoye.github.io/andns/).
